01 / Research

One question.
Three connected directions.

How can we understand a viewer’s experience—and keep that understanding reliable as conditions change?

01▂ ▅ ▃ ▇ ▄

Read the signals.

Infer playback quality from encrypted network traffic: startup delay, stalls, and selected bitrate, without reading the video payload.

Encrypted streaming ↗
02◎

Understand the person.

Connect facial emotion, viewer feedback, advertising, and network measurements to explain video quality and engagement.

Multimodal experience ↗
03↻

Adapt to change.

Detect when predictions drift, select recovery actions, and explain the response through the QoE-Foresight framework.
